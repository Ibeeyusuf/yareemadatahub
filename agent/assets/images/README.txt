Logo Files:
-----------
- logo.png: SVG version of the Yareema logo (scalable)
- logo.png: Place your PNG logo here (recommended size: 400x400px or higher)

The application will automatically fall back to an icon if logo.png is not found.

To add your custom logo:
1. Replace logo.png with your company logo
2. Recommended format: PNG with transparent background
3. Recommended size: 400x400px to 800x800px
4. The logo will be automatically resized to fit
