Privacy Policy

Yareema Data Hub Limited collects and uses personal data only to deliver our services, secure your account, improve your experience, and comply with applicable laws and regulations.

What We Collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, phone number, and login credentials.
  • Transaction details including service type, phone number, meter number, smartcard number, payment amount, and transaction timestamps.
  • Wallet funding history, payment confirmation details, and billing references.
  • Security and authentication information such as Transaction PINs, device metadata, and activity logs.

How We Use Your Data

  • To process and confirm your transactions.
  • To secure your account and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To improve the services and ensure regulatory compliance.

Account Deletion Requests

If you want to request deletion of your Yareema Data Hub account and all associated data, please use our dedicated account deletion page or contact support.

Delete account URL: https://www.yareemadatahub.com/delete-account.html

This page explains the steps to request deletion and the types of data that will be removed or retained.

Data Deleted

  • Account profile information.
  • Contact details and registered phone numbers.
  • Saved wallet information and payment references.
  • Transaction history and service usage records associated with the account.
  • Security credentials and Transaction PIN data.

Data Retained

Certain information may be retained for legal, regulatory, or fraud prevention purposes. This includes:

  • Financial audit logs and transaction references required by law.
  • Fraud prevention records, retained for up to 12 months.
  • Aggregated anonymized data used for service improvement, which does not identify you personally.
